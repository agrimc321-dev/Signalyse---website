§ ABOUT

Alex Morgan, founder.

Signalyse is built on experience in data analytics — principally the work of building the neural network that forecasts national electricity demand against the weather for a UK power company.

The work that earned its keep at the power company turned out to apply just as cleanly to a Bristol café as to the grid. Read the past closely. Factor in the weather. Predict the week ahead. The variables change. The maths doesn’t. The first sample report on this site is the energy work, kept here as the pedigree piece.

Signalyse is independent on purpose. When you hire us, you’re hiring me. You’re not getting an account manager, a junior analyst, or a templated dashboard. Every report is written, not generated. Every recommendation comes with the reasoning visible. That’s the point.

I’m based in Bristol. Most engagements start with a coffee, in person. The rest of the country comes later — and only when there’s a Bristol case study substantial enough to travel on.

§ 01  ·  WHY SIGNALYSE EXISTS

Small businesses deserve the same tools the big ones use.

Energy companies and large retailers have whole teams reading their data. They’ve had the benefit of demand forecasting, footfall modelling, and weather-adjusted pricing for years. Most small businesses can’t afford that — yet the impact of getting it right is, proportionally, much larger for them.

Signalyse exists to close that gap. The same techniques, scoped down, written up, and delivered at a price that pays for itself in stock control alone.

§ 02  ·  WHAT MAKES SIGNALYSE DIFFERENT

Three things to know before you hire us.

We write, we don’t dashboard.

Most analytics services hand you tools. We hand you conclusions. The deliverable is a written report — read it, decide, act.

Local first, in person.

Bristol-based by design. Engagements start with a coffee, not a Zoom call. The pace of the work matches the pace of your business.

No lock-in.

Every engagement starts with a one-off report. If the work isn’t useful, you walk away with the report and we shake hands. The standing relationship is earned, not contracted.
