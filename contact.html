§ 02  ·  THE PROCESS

From first meeting to finished report.

Every Signalyse engagement follows the same five steps. The whole thing, from handshake to delivered report, takes three to four weeks.

01

A 20-minute conversation.

We meet, in person if you’re in Bristol. No data shared yet. I show you a real report, you tell me what’s on your mind, and we decide together whether Signalyse is the right fit.

STEP 01  ·  20 MIN
02

The NDA goes first.

Before any data changes hands, you have a signed NDA. Identifying fields are stripped from your data within 24 hours of receipt. Raw exports are deleted as soon as the anonymised dataset is built.

STEP 02  ·  DAY 1
03

Bringing the data together.

Till exports, rotas, supplier invoices, social stats, weather, calendar — whatever’s relevant. I can sit with you and pull the till data point-by-point if exporting it yourself is more work than it’s worth.

STEP 03  ·  WEEK 1
04

The analysis and the write-up.

Two to three weeks of work. I’m reading your data closely and writing what I find. The report is the deliverable, the prose is the product, the recommendations come with their reasoning attached.

STEP 04  ·  WEEKS 2–3
05

A walk-through, in person.

We sit down with the finished report. I take you through every finding. You ask anything. From there it’s your call whether the relationship continues — and if it doesn’t, you keep the report and the recommendations.

STEP 05  ·  WEEK 3–4
§ 03  ·  YOUR DATA, HANDLED PROPERLY

Confidentiality is the contract before the contract.

Every Signalyse engagement begins with a signed mutual NDA. That happens before a single number is shared. We treat your data — and your customers’ data — with the same care we’d want for our own business.

Download our standard NDA (PDF)

  • NDA before any data is shared.

    A standard mutual NDA, signed digitally. Available to read before our first meeting.

  • Identifying fields stripped within 24 hours.

    Customer names, card numbers, anything personally identifying — removed before analysis begins.

  • Encrypted, access-controlled storage.

    Your data sits on a single encrypted volume only I have access to.

  • Raw exports deleted.

    Once the anonymised dataset is built, the original exports are removed.

  • Deletable on request, within 30 days.

    Walk away, and your data walks with you.
